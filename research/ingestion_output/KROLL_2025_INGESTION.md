## 10.2 Incident Response Patterns

### Kroll Frontline Observations

**Kroll's Cyber Threat Intelligence Team:**
- 3,000+ incidents handled annually
- 100,000+ hours of risk assessments and testing
- Frontline experience with crypto-related cyber incidents

**Key Insights from Incident Response:**

1. **Human-Centric Attacks:**
   - Phishing remains primary vector
   - Social engineering sophistication increasing
   - Email compromise most costly

2. **Exploitation of Unpatched Vulnerabilities:**
   - MS17-010 (EternalBlue) still exploited
   - Legacy Windows vulnerabilities persistent
   - Public-facing applications targeted

3. **Valid Account Abuse:**
   - Compromised credentials widely used
   - Lateral movement through trusted accounts
   - Difficult to detect

4. **Cryptocurrency Intersection:**
   - Crypto payments in ransomware
   - Digital asset theft acceleration
   - Money laundering through crypto
