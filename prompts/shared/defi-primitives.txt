## Common DeFi Protocol Patterns

### AMM (Automated Market Maker)
- **Constant product:** x * y = k (Uniswap V2 style)
- **Concentrated liquidity:** Uniswap V3 style with tick ranges
- **Stable swaps:** Curve-style with amplification parameter
- **Common vulnerabilities:** Price manipulation via reserve ratio, sandwich attacks, LP token inflation

### Lending/Borrowing
- **Overcollateralized:** Aave, Compound style with collateral factors
- **Interest rate models:** Utilization-based (jump rate, kinked)
- **Liquidation mechanics:** Health factor threshold triggers
- **Common vulnerabilities:** Oracle manipulation for liquidation, bad debt from price crashes, interest rate manipulation

### Vaults/Yield Aggregators
- **Share-based accounting:** deposit(assets) -> shares, withdraw(shares) -> assets
- **Strategy pattern:** Vault deploys funds to yield strategies
- **Common vulnerabilities:** Share price manipulation (first depositor attack), strategy loss socialization, sandwich on deposit/withdraw

### Staking
- **Reward distribution:** Per-block/per-second reward accumulation
- **Reward per token:** rewardPerToken = accumulated + (timeDelta * rewardRate / totalStaked)
- **Common vulnerabilities:** Reward draining via stake/unstake timing, division by zero when totalStaked = 0

### Bridges
- **Lock and mint:** Lock on source chain, mint wrapped on destination
- **Message passing:** Relayer-based with signature verification
- **Common vulnerabilities:** Message replay, insufficient signature validation, relayer manipulation

### Governance
- **Token-weighted voting:** Votes proportional to token balance
- **Timelock execution:** Proposals queued with delay before execution
- **Common vulnerabilities:** Flash loan governance attacks, proposal front-running, timelock bypass
